Evidence, limits, and raw numbers

What the recommendation rests on, how sure we are, and what would change the call.

  • If ExampleOrg accepts a full day of downtime for a whole site, Option B wins on cost and simplicity. Today the full-site ransomware recovery target is 12 hours.
  • If site bandwidth rises to 1 Gbps at most sites, Option B's restore times drop under target in four of five scenarios. Two carriers have quoted this for 2027.
  • If ExampleOrg hires an in-house systems administrator, Option C's labor cost mostly disappears and it becomes the cheapest design by a wide margin.
  • If appliance pricing at the year 4 refresh rises more than 40%, Option A's premium over B passes $75K. Re-run the model before signing a five-year term.
  • If the cloud replica region shares a disaster zone with the sites, Option A loses its site-loss advantage. The replica must stay in a separate region.
